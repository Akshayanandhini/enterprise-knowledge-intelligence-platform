# Employee Handbook

## Purpose

The .MDAI Employee Handbook establishes the foundational policies, procedures, and expectations governing employment relationships. This document serves as the primary reference for all employees regarding workplace standards, benefits, and organizational culture.

## Scope

This handbook applies to all full-time, part-time, and contract employees of .MDAI, including those working remotely, in offices, or in hybrid arrangements. Temporary employees and interns are subject to a subset of policies as defined in their employment agreements.

## Code of Conduct

### Professional Standards

All employees must maintain professional behavior and treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect. This includes:

- Punctuality and attendance in accordance with departmental schedules
- Clear communication within team hierarchies and cross-functional groups
- Adherence to confidentiality obligations regarding proprietary information
- Prohibition of harassment, discrimination, or intimidation in any form

Violations of these standards may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Refer to **Information Security Policy** (DOC-ISP-001) for confidentiality obligations and data handling requirements.

### Remote Work Guidelines

Employees working remotely must:

- Maintain a secure home network aligned with security requirements outlined in the Information Security Policy
- Ensure VPN usage for all access to company systems
- Participate in synchronous team meetings during designated core hours (9 AM - 12 PM PT)
- Report connectivity issues promptly to IT Support

Remote workers are entitled to flexible scheduling outside core hours provided deliverables meet agreed-upon deadlines. Hybrid arrangements require manager approval and formalized schedules.

## Compensation and Benefits

### Salary and Payroll

Employees receive bi-weekly compensation via direct deposit. Salary reviews occur annually in March. All compensation is confidential and should not be discussed with non-HR personnel in detail.

### Health Benefits

.MDAI provides comprehensive health insurance coverage including medical, dental, and vision plans. Benefits commence on the first day of employment. Employees may enroll dependents within 30 days of hire. Open enrollment occurs annually in November.

### Paid Time Off

- Employees accrue 20 days of paid vacation annually
- 10 paid holidays are observed company-wide
- 5 days of sick leave are provided annually
- Unused vacation may be carried forward up to 10 days to the following year
- PTO requests must be submitted through the HR system with manager approval

### Professional Development

.MDAI allocates $2,000 annually per employee for professional development including courses, certifications, and conference attendance. Requests are reviewed by the employee's manager and department head. Employees pursuing certifications related to role responsibilities may receive partial or full reimbursement contingent upon successful completion.

## Termination and Separation

### Voluntary Termination

Employees resigning should provide at least two weeks' written notice to their manager and HR. During the notice period, employees must ensure knowledge transfer and documentation. Failure to provide adequate notice may impact final compensation and references.

### Involuntary Termination

Employment may be terminated at-will for cause or without cause. Cause termination includes but is not limited to violations of this handbook, gross negligence, or breach of confidentiality. Severance is provided based on tenure as follows:

- Less than 1 year: 1 week of severance
- 1-3 years: 4 weeks of severance
- 3+ years: 8 weeks of severance plus extended benefits eligibility

### Offboarding Procedures

Upon termination, employees must:

- Return all company equipment including laptops, phones, and badges
- Disable system access and archive email data per retention policies
- Complete exit interviews documenting contractor satisfaction and feedback
- Sign necessary non-compete and confidentiality agreements

Access revocation follows procedures documented in the Deployment Runbook (DOC-DR-001) to ensure systems security.

## Workplace Safety and Incident Reporting

All workplace incidents, injuries, or safety concerns must be reported to HR within 24 hours. Incidents in production environments affecting customers should additionally follow the **Incident Response Playbook** (DOC-IRP-001).

Employee wellness is a priority. Employees experiencing mental health challenges have access to an Employee Assistance Program (EAP) providing confidential counseling services at no cost.

## Policy Amendments

This handbook is subject to revision at management discretion. Employees will receive notice of material changes via email and updated handbook versions. Continued employment constitutes acceptance of policy revisions.
