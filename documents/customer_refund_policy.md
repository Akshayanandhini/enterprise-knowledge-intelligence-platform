# Customer Refund Policy

## Purpose

The Customer Refund Policy establishes transparent guidelines for issuing refunds to .MDAI customers. This policy ensures consistent treatment of customer requests while protecting company financial integrity and maintaining customer trust.

## Scope

This policy applies to all subscription renewals, annual commitments, and one-time purchase refunds. Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the VP of Customer Success. The policy does not apply to professional services, which are governed by separate service agreements.

## Subscription Refund Terms

### Standard Refund Window

Customers may request refunds within 30 days of purchase for the following reasons:

- Service did not meet documented feature specifications
- Service was unavailable for more than 8 cumulative hours in a billing month
- Billing error resulting in overcharge
- Customer decision to discontinue service

Refunds requested after 30 days are evaluated on a case-by-case basis by the Customer Success team.

### Partial Period Refunds

If a customer upgrades or downgrades mid-month, the following calculation applies:

Refund = (Days Remaining / Days in Billing Cycle) × (Amount Paid for Downgraded Plan - Current Month Charge)

Partial refunds are processed within 5 business days of approval.

### No Refund Scenarios

Refunds are not issued for:

- Customers exceeding documented API rate limits
- Use cases not within the licensed product scope
- Failures resulting from customer misconfiguration
- Services cancelled after 30-day refund window
- Customers with outstanding support tickets or open disputes

## Annual Plan Refunds

Customers purchasing annual plans receive the following refund terms:

- Within 30 days: 100% refund minus 10% cancellation fee
- Days 31-90: 75% refund minus 10% cancellation fee
- Days 91-365: 50% refund, no cancellation fee applied
- After 365 days: No refund available

Annual plan customers are entitled to service credits equal to unused time at no additional charge as an alternative to refunds. This credit option is preferred for customer retention.

## Refund Processing and Payment

### Approval Workflow

1. Customer submits refund request via support ticket or billing contact
2. Support team verifies eligibility against this policy
3. Technical validation confirms stated reason (e.g., unavailability documentation)
4. Customer Success manager reviews and approves or denies
5. Finance processes approved refund within 5 business days

### Refund Methods

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. For credit card payments, refunds appear within 3-5 business days depending on the issuing bank. ACH transfers and wire refunds process within 2-3 business days.

Customers may request alternative refund methods by contacting billing@mdai.com. Wire transfer refunds incur a $25 processing fee deducted from the refund amount.

### Dispute and Chargeback Resolution

If a customer initiates a chargeback without first attempting resolution through this policy, .MDAI will:

1. Respond to the chargeback with documentation of services provided
2. Refuse future chargebacks for 90 days by declining transactions
3. Restrict the customer's access to services during dispute period
4. Pursue restitution for chargeback fees incurred

Customers filing chargebacks are not eligible for future refunds and may be terminated from service.

## Refund and Billing Integration

Refund decisions must account for customer usage metrics. High-usage customers requesting refunds within the window may indicate a legitimate service issue and warrant approval. Conversely, customers with minimal usage may indicate trial behavior outside the refund policy scope.

Support teams coordinate with Finance to review customer invoicing history when evaluating refund requests. Billing corrections are processed via the Deployment Runbook (DOC-DR-001) when infrastructure-level billing adjustments are required.

## Executive Escalation

Refund requests exceeding $50,000 or involving strategic accounts require escalation to the Chief Financial Officer for approval. Customers at risk of churn may be offered extended payment terms or service credits rather than refunds.

## Communication Standards

Customers receive written confirmation of refund decisions within 2 business days of processing. Denials include specific policy references and alternative options such as service credits or support escalation.

## Policy Amendments and Customer Communication

Changes to this policy are announced 60 days in advance via email and posted to the customer support portal. Existing customers retain the refund terms in effect at their subscription start date unless explicitly agreeing to new terms.
